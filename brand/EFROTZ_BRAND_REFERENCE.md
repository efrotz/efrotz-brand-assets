has already been exported in each social platform's exact pixel dimensions.

- Muted Gold: #D4AF37

- Mascot: nonhuman ivory-hooded character, black face, warm crescent eyes,
  crimson ribbons, gold trim, circular sparkle emblem, playful pointing pose.
- Final wordmark preference: the Z must NOT have a long underline tail.
- Style: playful without being childish, not corporate, no black/neon-green hacker aesthetic.

## HOW TO USE
- The master brand board is the primary reference for the mascot, palette, and wordmark.
- The avatar master is for profile icons; the 400px file is a smaller convenience export.
- The transparent files are cutouts. Preserve alpha when editing.
- Banner graphics may still require platform-specific crop or sizing checks.
- The thumbnail background is only a reusable starting asset, not an approved final thumbnail.
- Avoid silently redesigning the mascot or substituting one of the rejected concepts.
