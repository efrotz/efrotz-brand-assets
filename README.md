# Efrotz brand assets

Original approved Efrotz artwork for Discord, community integrations, and social media. The ten supplied PNGs, contact sheet, and brand reference are preserved byte-for-byte. The pre-existing 400px avatar is identical to the supplied export and remains at its existing path.

## Identity

**Efrotz · Ideas Drift Further.** Locked palette: charcoal `#1F1F24`, crimson `#D7263D`, gold `#D4AF37`, ivory `#F7F4E8`. Preserve the original ivory-hooded mascot and wordmark. The Z must not acquire a long underline tail. Do not redraw, recolor, stretch, or silently replace approved artwork.

## Structure and approval status

- `approved/`: unchanged approved original artwork, organized by use.
- `brand/`: verbatim brand reference and exact CSS/JSON palette transcriptions.
- `proposed/`: new supporting artwork awaiting owner review; never imply approval.
- `docs/`: hosting instructions and verification notes.
- `asset-inventory.json`: original filenames, dimensions, byte sizes, and SHA-256 checksums.
